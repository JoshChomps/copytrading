Caveats

  • Congress amounts are ranges, never exact sizes; options are excluded from backtests (unpriceable from a range disclosure), so a filer whose edge is leverage looks weaker here.
  • Unpriceable/delisted tickers are excluded and counted, results are upward-biased to the extent losers delist.
  • Decay curves are gross of costs; equity curves and tables are net.
  • The copy basket holds a little uninvested cash between signals and that cash earns nothing; measured mean invested weight is ~97%, so the drag is roughly 0.3-0.5%/yr against a 100%-invested ETF. It is a real part of the gap, not the whole of it.
  • Sharpe/Sortino/Treynor use a 0% risk-free rate for every row, so they are comparable with each other but are not absolute risk-adjusted returns.
  • Equity curves are pre-tax. A high-turnover book in a margin sleeve pays annually; after-tax curves are not modelled.
  • Research tooling only: nothing here places orders or is investment advice.
